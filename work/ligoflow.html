The problem

Decisions get made in meetings and chat threads, then the follow-up gets lost. LigoFlow connects to the tools a team already talks in, reads the conversation, and turns it into tasks, action items and insights, so context becomes execution.

What I built

  • Meeting analyzer that processes conversations and pulls out what needs to happen.
  • Automatic task generation from discussions, pushed into the team's task management tools.
  • Integrations with communication and meeting tools.
  • Subscription billing through Lemon Squeezy.
  • Next.js interface over a Django API, with AI model integrations behind it.

Technical choices

  • Celery runs the heavy work, such as analysis and generation, in the background so the interface stays fast.
  • Next.js serves the product UI, and Django owns the data, integrations and business rules.
  • AI calls sit behind a backend service layer, so the models can change without touching the UI.
  • Lemon Squeezy handles billing and tax, which keeps payments code out of the product.
